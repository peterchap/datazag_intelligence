# Cross-estate (portfolio) report — handover

*Written 2026-07-02. The Analytics-MVP build of the cross-estate report:
an aggregation layer over the per-domain `ReportViewModel`. The per-domain
pipeline is unchanged.*

## What this is

`crossestate/` consumes N per-domain contracts and computes the findings that
only exist at N>1, then renders an exception-first "summary-of-summaries".
Governing principle: **aggregation, not concatenation** — lead with the
aggregate and the exceptions; per-domain detail is a drill-down appendix.
Findings are rule-based, never LLM-invented (same discipline as the single
report).

Spec: the "Cross-Estate Report Specification" (portfolio product). This build
covers the 5 deterministic analytics (§2.2–2.6); estate completeness (§2.1) and
discovery triage (§2.7) are stubbed (`discovery.NullDiscoveryProvider`) — they
need a 330M-corpus + Companies House CRN + connected-domain infra not in this
repo.

## Data flow

    manifest (JSON/CSV) ──load_manifest──▶ ManifestEntry[]
        └─ load_contract(path) ▶ ReportViewModel   (view-model dump; medallion fallback)
    resolve_segments(tags + inference) ▶ DomainRef[]
    5 analytics ▶ blocks ─▶ EstateViewModel ─▶ derive_estate_exceptions
    CrossEstateRenderer(estate, cut) ▶ to_json / to_markdown / to_html

## The operator | oversight cut

The cut parameterises the HUMAN render only. **JSON is always complete and
cut-independent** (`model_dump`), including per-domain remediation.
- **operator** — leads with the exception register + calendar; per-domain fixes
  shown; appendix = per-domain drill-down.
- **oversight** (insurer/board) — leads with concentration/variance/exposure;
  per-domain fix instructions **suppressed** (liability artefact); appendix =
  **fixable-weakness rollup** ("62% of the estate shares this fixable weakness").
Suppression is render-time (`show_per_domain_fixes`), never mutates the model.

### Why it parses the raw SPF record instead of reading the lake

`gold.dns_wide.spf_includes` is built with an `[a-z0-9._-]+` class that silently drops every
**macro-bearing** include. That is how the enterprise tier publishes SPF delegation —
`include:%{ir}.%{v}.%{d}.spf.has.pphosted.com`. On the 18-domain `portfolio_test` estate,
5 domains delegate that way; a layer built on the corpus column would have reported "no email
gateway observed" for exactly the population these reports are sold into.

### Coverage on a real estate (Meridian Holdings, 18 domains, 2026-08-24)

Observable on **15 of 18** (83%) — the other three publish only self-hosted `_spf.<own-domain>`
shards, which are excluded from the denominator rather than scored. Top vendor **Proofpoint,
47% of 15 → flagged**, emitted as `technographic_concentration` with 7 domain `members`.

### Two behaviours that are load-bearing, not decoration

1. **Withholding.** `dimension_min_coverage_pct` applies to **every** dimension, not just this
   one. On the estate above it immediately caught four pre-existing dimensions —
   `registrar`, `asn`, `hosting`, `ca_issuer` — at **0% coverage**, which previously rendered
   as "top provider —, 0%, not flagged" and read as *no concentration*. They now render as
   *withheld*, with a note saying it is not a finding of absence. (That estate was collected
   without RDAP/cert intel; the dimensions were empty, not clean.)
2. 🚨 **The impersonation join ranks, it never suppresses.** `in_estate_stack` is applied
   after totals and ordering are fixed, and `stack_matched_30d` is carried parallel to
   `total_30d`, never subtracted. Pinned by two tests in
   `tests/test_estate_technographic.py`, including one that strips the estate's entire stack
   and asserts every exposure number is byte-identical. A non-match is not safety, and
   suppression would inherit every coverage gap in `technographic.py` as a silent false
   negative.

### `platform_signals` was dead — reconnected

`Annotation.platform_signals` was declared on the contract and consumed by
`healthreport/renderer.py` and `freereport/compose.py`, but **produced by nothing**:
`lake_enrich._labels_fallback` reproduces `v_annotated` per-domain and never reproduced
`v_technographic`. Verified empty on all 18 contracts of a collected estate. Now populated in
`to_view_models` using the same matcher (`crossestate.technographic.signals_for`) against the
live SPF record already on `rec`.

### Not shipped, deliberately

- **No SAN-derived dimension.** See `dnsproject/scripts/tech_san_lookup.py` — the cert
  archive's `apex_domain` is one value per certificate, so vendor SANs are filed under the
  *vendor's* apex. Over a 600k-row sample, SANs under 23 known vendor domains resolved to the
  vendor's own apex in all but two cases; over the 18-domain estate the per-apex path
  returned zero vendor attributions.
- **Materiality does not render yet.** `docs/DILIGENCE_CUT_SPEC.md`'s `limit` weighting
  (`DomainRef.limit`, `EstateException.materiality_pct`, the `diligence` cut) is **still a
  spec — none of it is implemented**. The technographic exception is emitted `scope="domain"`
  with `members` so it inherits materiality the moment that lands; the *"affects N% of
  portfolio value"* line cannot render before then.
